# Imvault 0.20.5

Make an album while you sort. In your gallery, select photos, open **Add to
album**, choose **Create a new album**, enter a title and click **Add to
album**. The option is there even before you have any albums.

The album and its photos are saved together. If any selected photo cannot be
added, nothing is saved and no empty album is left behind. The new album
starts private, and only you manage its contributions. The photos keep their
visibility, metadata settings and other albums. Titles can be up to 120
characters. Your gallery search, sort, date filter and page stay put.

It works without JavaScript and on small screens. With JavaScript on, a missing
title keeps your selection and moves focus to the title field.

The bulk album API accepts `album: "new"` with `album_title` and still returns
`{"updated": N}`.

There is no database migration. Back up before upgrading, install the package
or image, and restart Imvault. See [installation](../releases.md) and
[containers](../containers.md).
