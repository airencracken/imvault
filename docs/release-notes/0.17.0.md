# Imvault 0.17.0

Tell people who you are. Under Settings → Account → Profile, add an optional
name, a plain-text bio and up to five labeled HTTP/HTTPS links. Your username
stays the same, and clearing a field removes it. Author links on files and
albums open profiles. Every profile needs sign-in to view, even for authors of
public uploads.

Profiles never list private files or albums, email, quotas or credentials.
Disabled and deleted accounts have no profile page. Deleting an account removes
its profile for good. Your account export includes your profile even if you
have no files.

Stop the service and back up the full data directory before upgrading, then
restart it. The upgrade adds empty profile storage.
