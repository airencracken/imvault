# Imvault 0.18.0

Optional avatars complete the member profile experience. Open Settings → Account
→ Profile to upload a PNG, JPEG, or GIF, or remove your picture. Avatars appear on
member profiles and beside the contributor on file pages when you are signed in.
Avatar images require sign-in even when the original file page is public.
Administrators can remove a member's avatar from that member's profile.

Image handling uses published Comfylib 0.1.5's profile-image normalizer. Inputs are
bounded to 2 MiB and 512 by 512 pixels, with at most 64 GIF frames. Images are
re-encoded without embedded metadata, and animations retain a separate PNG still
rendition. GIF frame delays are at least a tenth of a second.

Show animated avatars is a viewer setting under Profile. Disabling it serves
still PNGs; device reduced-motion settings also select the still rendition.
Your preference survives uploading, replacing, or removing an avatar. Save and
removal notices work with normal browser forms. Avatar changes do not change
names, bios, links, or the uploaded images in your gallery.

Account exports include your avatar.png, optional avatar.gif and animation
preference. They never include another account's avatar. Account deletion removes
pictures and preferences; backups and restores preserve them.

Startup applies migration 032 atomically, leaving accounts and profiles intact.
Stop the service and take a backup before upgrading. An older binary cannot open
the upgraded database; restore the pre-upgrade backup to revert.
