# Imvault 0.18.0

Put a face to the name. Open Settings → Account → Profile to upload a PNG, JPEG
or GIF avatar, or remove it. Avatars appear on member profiles and beside the
contributor on file pages, for signed-in people only, even when the file page
is public. Administrators can remove a member's avatar from that member's
profile.

Avatars can be up to 2 MiB and 512 by 512 pixels, with at most 64 GIF frames.
Imvault re-encodes them without embedded metadata and keeps a still PNG of each
animation. GIF frames last at least a tenth of a second.

**Show animated avatars**, under Profile, is your viewing preference. Turn it
off to see still pictures; a reduced-motion setting on your device does the
same. The preference survives uploading, replacing or removing your own avatar.
Changing your avatar does not touch your name, bio, links or gallery.

Account exports include your `avatar.png`, any `avatar.gif` and your animation
preference, never anyone else's avatar. Deleting an account removes its
pictures and preference. Backups and restores keep them.

Stop the service and back up before upgrading. Startup migrates the database;
an older binary cannot open it afterwards, so restore the pre-upgrade backup to
go back.
