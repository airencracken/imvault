# Imvault 0.20.0

## Browse by camera date

Sort your gallery by the date a photo was taken, and filter by year or month.
Photos without a camera date sit in an **Unknown** group. Imvault keeps the
camera's own clock time; it does not guess a timezone or fill in the upload
date. Date browsing covers only your own library.

## Act on a selection

Select photos in your gallery to add them to an album, change their visibility
or tag them. If any photo in the selection fails, nothing changes. Your filters
and page stay put. Everything works without JavaScript.

## Trash

Deleting your own upload moves it to a private Trash for 30 days. Its page and
links stop working at once. Restoring it brings back its sharing, tags,
favorites and albums. A trashed file still counts against your quota until you
delete it permanently or it expires. Account exports and backups include Trash.

When staff remove an upload, it is gone for good, even if the owner had already
moved it to Trash.

## API change

`DELETE /api/v1/files/{id}` on your own file now returns `status: "trashed"`.
To free the quota at once, follow it with `DELETE /api/v1/trash/{id}`. Staff
deleting someone else's upload still get `status: "deleted"`.

## Before upgrading

The upgrade adds Trash and an index of camera dates, filled in during the
upgrade. Take a verified backup with your current binary first. Older binaries
refuse the new schema; to go back, restore the pre-upgrade backup and run its
matching binary. Automatic backups made after startup do not replace that
backup.

See [organizing your library](../library.md) and the [API guide](../api.md).
